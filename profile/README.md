Self Talk brings the check-ins and self-talk of the athletes you work with into one place, finds the patterns that hold them back, and gets you ready for every session, from an app that lives on your Home Screen.

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>🗂️ Collect</h3>
      Every check-in in one place. Send a personal link and athletes answer on any phone, no account needed. Or invite them to the app to record training, matches and match analysis.
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>🔍 Understand</h3>
      Patterns in their own words. AI reads the entries and surfaces recurring thoughts and trends in effort and performance, with the athlete's own quotes as evidence.
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>🎯 Prepare</h3>
      Walk into every session ready. Each record gets a one-line summary, and every analysis includes a pre-session brief: what changed and what to raise next.
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <h3>🔒 Private by design</h3>
      Their words stay theirs. Each consultant sees only their own athletes, changes are logged, and names are stripped from entries before AI reads them, where we can detect them.
    </td>
  </tr>
